How to add to Gmail:
  1. Click Copy signature above
  2. Go to Gmail → Settings → General → Signature
  3. Select your signature from the list (or create a new one)
  4. Click inside the signature editor and press Cmd+V (Mac) or Ctrl+V (Windows)
  5. Under Signature defaults, set both dropdowns (For new emails and On reply/forward) to your signature
  6. Check "Insert signature before quoted text and remove the '--' line"
  7. Click Save Changes
